Last updated 6 November 2026

The short version

  • Relay keeps track of what you buy, using receipts from your email, and helps you with returns, swaps and price drops.
  • Relay reads information that shops send you, so it can sometimes be wrong. Check important dates with the shop.
  • Shops decide returns and refunds, not Relay. Relay never refunds you itself.
  • If you win a shop's auction, you pay the shop within 24 hours at its own checkout. Relay never takes the money.
  • You can stop using Relay and delete your account at any time.

1. About these terms

These terms apply when you use Relay. By creating an account, you agree to them. Our Privacy policy explains what we do with your information.

2. Your account

  • You need to be 18 or over to use Relay.
  • Your account is for you alone. Keep access to your email, and any passkeys or authenticator app, secure, because they're how you sign in.
  • Tell us straight away if you think someone else has got into your account.

3. Connecting your email

Relay is most useful with your email connected. When you connect it, you give Relay permission to read it in read-only mode to find your receipts, as described on the permission screen and in our Privacy policy. You can disconnect it at any time.

4. What Relay can and can't do

  • Relay works out your purchases, return deadlines and return instructions from emails shops send you. Emails vary, so Relay can miss a purchase or read a detail wrongly. Always check an important deadline with the shop.
  • Relay can remind you and show you how to return something, but it can't start a refund on its own. Refunds always come from the shop, under the shop's own returns policy.
  • Prices, stock and offers in Relay come from shops and can change at any time. Relay is not responsible for a shop's prices, products or delivery.

5. Returns and swaps through Relay

Some shops let you start a return or swap a size or colour through Relay. When you do, Relay passes your request to the shop, and the shop's own terms and returns policy apply. If a swap costs more, you'll see the difference and agree to it before you pay. That payment goes to the shop.

6. Swapping and selling with other members

Where it's available, you can list things to swap or sell to other Relay members. Any deal is between you and the other member, not Relay. Describe items honestly, only list things you own, and be respectful. We can remove listings and stop members using this feature if they break these terms or other members report a problem.

7. Shops' auctions

  • Shops on Relay can put clothing up for auction. The shop is the seller: it describes the item, sets the starting price and the delivery price, and sends it to the winner. Relay runs the bidding and never takes payment.
  • Your bid is the most you'd pay. Relay bids for you only as much as it takes to keep you ahead, up to that amount. A bid in the last two minutes gives everyone two more minutes.
  • If you win, you agree to pay the shop the winning price and the delivery price at its own checkout within 24 hours. If you don't, the order is cancelled and the item goes to the next bidder. Not paying for two wins in 90 days stops you bidding for 30 days.
  • Winning an auction on Relay is an online purchase from the shop, so your usual rights apply, including cancelling within 14 days of delivery. The shop's own terms and returns policy apply too.
  • Shops, their staff and anyone acting for them must not bid on their own auctions.

Please don't:

  • use someone else's email or account, or let someone use yours;
  • add fake or altered receipts, or use Relay to mislead a shop;
  • bid in an auction you don't mean to pay for, or bid on your own shop's auction (or get someone to do it for you);
  • try to get into parts of Relay or other people's data that aren't yours, or disrupt the service;
  • copy or collect data from Relay with automated tools.
