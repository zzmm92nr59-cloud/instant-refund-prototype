RelayBot

Last updated 5 November 2026

The short version

RelayBot is how Relay, a UK shopping app, reads shops' public product lists, so shoppers can see what's for sale. It reads only what your robots.txt allows, about once a day, and never anything about your customers.

What it reads

On shops built on Shopify, RelayBot asks for three public addresses, in this order, and nothing else:

  • /robots.txt, to see what it may read. If robots.txt doesn't allow the next two, it stops here.
  • /meta.json, for your shop's currency and the countries you deliver to. Relay shows only shops that sell in pounds and deliver to the UK.
  • /products.json, for your newest products: names, sizes and colours, prices, pictures, and whether each one can be bought right now.

It never signs in, adds anything to a basket, checks out, or reads customer accounts or orders.

How often

About once a day for each shop: three requests, one after another. If your website doesn't answer, it tries again about 6 hours later. If your robots.txt says no, it checks again after a week.

Where your products show

For now, only Relay's own team sees them, in a preview of the app's shopping page. Each product links to its page on your website, where it's bought. Relay shows the price and stock as your website showed them, with when Relay last checked, and hides a shop's products once they're 3 days old, so prices are never days out of date. Relay doesn't sell your products or take payments for them.

How to stop it

Add this to your robots.txt:

User-agent: RelayBot
Disallow: /

RelayBot reads robots.txt before anything else, every time. Once it says no, Relay removes your products the next time RelayBot visits.

How to recognise it

Every request RelayBot makes has this User-Agent:

RelayBot/1.0 (+https://zzmm92nr59-cloud.github.io/instant-refund-prototype/relaybot.html)
Shops can also join Relay, so shoppers can swap and return what they bought from you in the app. See also the Privacy policy.
