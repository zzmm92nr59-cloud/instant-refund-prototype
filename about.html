Relay

Every receipt, return date and parcel in one place

Relay is an app for people who shop in the UK. It finds the order confirmations and receipts in your email, and shows you what you bought, when each thing has to go back by, and where your parcels are.

Open Relay

What Relay does

  • Finds your receipts

    Connect Gmail or Outlook and Relay picks out your order confirmations and receipts, so every purchase is in one list.

  • Keeps track of return dates

    Relay shows the last day each thing can go back, and reminds you before it if you ask.

  • Follows your parcels

    See what's on its way and what has arrived, from the delivery emails shops send you.

  • Keeps return codes ready

    The barcode or QR code a shop emails you for a return or a collection is there when you need to show it.

Why Relay asks to read your email

Receipts arrive by email, so Relay can only find them if you let it look. When you connect Gmail, Google asks whether Relay may view your email messages. Relay asks for read-only access, and uses it only for what's on this page.

  • What it looks for

    Order confirmations, receipts, and delivery and return emails. Other emails are not kept.

  • What it keeps

    The shop, order number, date, total and items, the return details, one small product photo, a code to show or scan if the email has one, and a link back to the email. Not the email itself.

  • What it can't do

    Relay can't send email from your account, and can't delete, move or change anything in your inbox.

  • How emails are read

    By Relay's own rules, on Relay's servers. When those rules can't work out a receipt, Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, reads that one email with your name, addresses, links, phone numbers and card numbers hidden first. You can switch this off in Relay under AI help. Your emails are never used to train AI.

  • What never happens

    Relay never sells what it finds, never uses it for adverts, and nobody at Relay reads your email unless you choose to send one to Relay yourself.

  • Stopping

    Disconnect your email in Relay at any time and everything Relay captured from it is deleted. For Gmail, Relay also cancels its access at Google.

Relay's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The privacy policy says exactly what Relay does with information from Google.
