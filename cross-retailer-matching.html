Four customers, multiple orders, Square + Shopify + a third simulated retailer. Card registration and Plaid bank transactions are compared against each order. No real orders are assigned to wallets.

Matching scenario

Bank transactions are simulated with customer-linked Plaid account identifiers. Plaid transaction data does not supply a trustworthy POS card last four or exact time. Card registrations are self-declared, and all matches remain unverified.

What this establishes

The matching engine searches all bank transactions across all customers for each retailer order, compares amount, merchant, date and registered card brand/last four, and refuses to assign ambiguous or missing candidates. This is a logic test, not ownership verification. Actual Square payments, Shopify orders and Plaid Sandbox transactions must be separately fetched and reconciled before a real integration claim.

The third provider is a generic retailer adapter fixture, not a connected retailer. The actual Square Sandbox nonce previously used produces card 5858. Shopify test payments and card data depend on the configured gateway and API permissions.
