# Relay product roadmap

## Current priorities
1. Establish GitHub-to-Cloudflare Worker deployment.
2. Complete controlled Square Sandbox → Plaid Sandbox → verified Relay wallet purchase test.
3. Build a richer North & Co Square Sandbox catalogue and reason-aware returns/retention demonstration (instant cash before return, size exchanges, alternative products, enhanced store credit).

## Later concept: Relay Community / Relay Exchange
Peer-to-peer circular commerce within the Relay app, inspired by Vinted.

- Allow users to list purchases already in their Relay wallet, with product details pre-populated.
- Swap clothes, shoes and other eligible items with other customers.
- Enable peer-to-peer resale and swaps plus cash differences.
- Browse by brand, size, category, condition and other attributes.
- Consider a "Swap with another Relay user" option alongside refund, retailer exchange and store credit where appropriate.
- Investigate tracked shipping, disputes, identity verification, fraud prevention, payment escrow/settlement and item-condition verification.

This is a future hypothesis, not part of the immediate MVP. Validate demand and operating economics before building.
