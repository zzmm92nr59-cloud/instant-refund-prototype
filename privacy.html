Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

The short version

  • Relay only reads your email if you connect it, and only in read-only mode. It looks for order confirmations, receipts and return emails, and nothing else.
  • It keeps the details of each purchase (store, items, total, return deadline) and a link back to the email. It does not keep the emails themselves.
  • Your emails are read by Relay's own rules on Relay's own servers. No AI reads them, and they are never sent to an AI company.
  • Relay never sells your data, never shows you ads, and has no tracking or analytics.
  • You can disconnect your email, withdraw your permission or delete your account at any time, from the Account page.

What we collect

Your account

The email address you sign in with. When you sign in, we keep a record that your session is active (stored in scrambled form) so you stay signed in.

What you choose to add

  • Your name and profile photo, if you add them. Only you see them.
  • Receipts you paste in yourself.
  • Which receipts were gifts, and who each was for if you say. Only you see them: they show in your receipts and your Wrapped, and a gift isn't used to work out your own style.
  • Products and shops you ask us to watch for price drops, and your alert settings.
  • Passkeys (Face ID, Touch ID or screen lock sign-in). We keep the public part of the key, a name for the device and when it was last used. Your fingerprint or face never leaves your device.
  • A mobile number, if you add one. We keep it encrypted, show it in the app only as its last three digits (Download my data gives you the whole number), and use it only to text you sign-in codes: never for marketing, and never shown to anyone else.
  • Two-step sign-in. We keep your authenticator key encrypted, and your recovery codes and any codes we text you only in scrambled form.
  • A card for swaps, if you save one. The card is handled by Stripe; we keep only its brand, last four digits and expiry date.
  • If you list something to swap or sell to other members: the listing, your messages with other members, and the first half of your postcode so people can see roughly how far away it is.

Purchases from shops connected to Relay

Some shops connect to Relay through Shopify or Square. When one of them confirms an order of yours, we keep its items and amounts so it appears in your account. For in-store purchases, we may keep a scrambled code made from the card you paid with (never the card number) along with its brand and last four digits, so we can suggest which purchases are yours. When you start a return or swap through Relay, we keep a record of it, including the item, the reason and any tracking number.

Technical information

To stop people repeatedly trying to sign in to accounts, we keep a scrambled form of your IP address with each sign-in request for about a day. When we text a code, we keep a scrambled form of the account, the phone number and the IP address for about two days, so nobody can use Relay to flood a phone with texts; this stays for those two days even if the account is deleted. Our hosting provider keeps short-term logs of requests to Relay's servers for running and fixing the service.

On your device

Relay stores a few things in your browser so it works quickly: your sign-in, a copy of your receipts so they show straight away, and settings like your price alerts. We don't use cookies for advertising or tracking. Signing out removes your sign-in and the copy of your receipts from that device.

Your email

If you connect Gmail or Outlook, you give Relay read-only access. It cannot send, delete, move or change anything in your inbox.

  • What it looks for: order confirmations, receipts and return emails. Other emails are not kept.
  • What it keeps from each one: the store, order number, date, total and items; the email address the shop sent the order to (so a returns page gets the right one); return details such as the deadline, how to return, any return ID or link; one small product photo from the email if there is one; and a link back to the original email so you can open it yourself.
  • How it reads them: with fixed rules that run on Relay's own servers. Unless you switch on AI help, no AI model reads your emails and they are never sent to an AI company. Your emails are never used to train an AI model.
  • AI help (off unless you switch it on): under Email connection. When Relay's rules can't work out a receipt, Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, reads that email to fill in what's missing. It gets the email's text with web links, email addresses, phone numbers, card numbers and codes, postcodes, addresses and your name hidden. Anthropic's terms don't let it train its models on what Relay sends. What the AI fills in is marked in Relay for you to check, never replaces what you corrected yourself, and is deleted when you switch AI help off.
  • How far back: about the last 16 months. If you ask for a Wrapped of an earlier year, Relay looks through that year as well.
  • Keeping up to date: while your email is connected, Relay checks for new receipts every so often in the background.
  • The key to your inbox: stored encrypted, and used only to look for receipts.
  • Sending an email to Relay: only if you choose to, from a receipt Relay read wrong. You see exactly what will be sent first. Relay hides web links, email addresses, phone numbers, card numbers and codes, postcodes, addresses and your name before it's sent, and you can add a note. A person at Relay reads it to work out why it was read wrong and improve how Relay reads emails like it. They don't see who sent it, and each time one is opened it's recorded. It's kept encrypted, and deleted after 30 days, or sooner if you delete it, remove the receipt, disconnect that inbox, withdraw your permission or delete your account.
  • More than one inbox: you can connect up to five, Gmail or Outlook. Each is read in the same way, and disconnecting one deletes the receipts Relay captured from it.

Relay's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

How we use it

  • To show your purchases, return deadlines and what you can still return or swap.
  • To send you a return reminder, but only for items where you ask for one. It goes once, to your sign-in address.
  • To send price alerts and other notifications to your phone, only if you turn them on. A shop can also send an offer on something you're watching; it reaches you as an alert, and the shop never learns who received it.
  • To build your Wrapped from your own receipts.
  • To check how well Relay reads receipts, using counts across everyone (for example, how often a total was read right). When a shop connected to Relay confirms an order of yours, we compare it with what Relay read from that shop's email to you about the same order. Nobody at Relay reads your emails for this, and it changes nothing in your account.
  • To keep your account secure.

We use your data because you've asked us to provide Relay to you, and, for reading your email, because you've given us your permission. We do not sell it, use it for advertising or share it with data brokers.

Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect your email: Account, then Email connection. This deletes every receipt Relay captured from that inbox. For Gmail, Relay also cancels its access at Google.
  • Withdraw your permission: Account, then Privacy & data. This disconnects your email and deletes what Relay captured.
  • Get a copy of your data: Account, then Download my data. You get one file with everything Relay keeps about you.
  • Delete your account: Account, then Delete my account. This removes your sign-in, email connection, receipts, profile, price alerts, passkeys, two-step sign-in, saved card, and your swap and sell listings and messages. For Gmail, Relay also cancels its access at Google. If you connected Outlook, you can also remove Relay from the apps connected to your Microsoft account.
  • Turn off notifications: on the Price alerts page, or in your phone's settings.

You also have the right to ask for a copy of your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to how we use it, and to complain to a data protection regulator. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office, at ico.org.uk.

Keeping it safe

The keys to your inbox and your two-step sign-in are encrypted. Sessions, recovery codes and sign-in links are only stored in scrambled form. Everything travels over encrypted connections.

Children

Relay is meant for adults. Please don't use it if you're under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change what Relay collects or how it's used, we'll update this page and tell you in the app. If the change affects what Relay reads from your email, we'll ask for your permission again first.
