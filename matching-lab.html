Matching safeguards

A card's last four digits are user-declared, not verified by Plaid. Matching them to Square's card details adds a useful independent comparison but is not enough to establish ownership. A real wallet must also check merchant, amount, timing, location, collisions across customers, and obtain stronger verification before exposing itemised orders.
