2. Run matching scenarios

Every bank transaction is independently associated with its own Plaid Sandbox customer account, but Plaid supplies no card digits or precise timestamp in the observed test. The matching engine compares all four customer bank records against all four POS fixtures, not against a preassigned customer.

3. What this proves

The demo tests candidate selection, card-last-four comparisons, duplicate collisions and refusal to assign ambiguous purchases. It does not establish real-world payment ownership: the POS last-four digits and bank transaction timestamps are simulated, the card registrations are customer-declared, and the actual Square Sandbox payments all used card 5858. Even a unique candidate is shown as unverified, not released to a wallet.
